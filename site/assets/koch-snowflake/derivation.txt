Koch snowflake: finite area and infinite perimeter

Start with an equilateral triangle of side length s and area A_0=sqrt(3)s^2/4.
At each step, replace every boundary segment with four segments, each one third as long.

Segment count at stage n:
    m_n = 3*4^n
Segment length:
    l_n = s/3^n
Perimeter:
    P_n = 3s*(4/3)^n
Hence P_n tends to infinity.

At step n to n+1, the new triangles have side length s/3^(n+1).
Their combined area is:
    Delta A_(n+1) = (A_0/3)*(4/9)^n
Therefore:
    A_n = A_0*(8/5 - (3/5)*(4/9)^n)
    A_infinity = (8/5)*A_0 = (2sqrt(3)/5)*s^2

The Koch boundary contains four similar copies at scale 1/3.
The similarity exponent satisfies 4*(1/3)^d=1, so d=log(4)/log(3), approximately 1.26186.
For this boundary the exponent agrees with its Hausdorff dimension.

The length calculation concerns the boundary. The finite-area calculation concerns its enclosed region.
