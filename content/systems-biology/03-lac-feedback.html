LacY transport and positive feedback

With external input fixed, the starting amounts of internal inducer and transport protein can determine whether expression settles at a low or high level.

TMG is an inducer, a molecule that reduces repression of the lac genes. External TMG surrounds the cell; internal TMG is the amount taken up. LacY is the membrane protein that transports it. More LacY increases uptake, and more internal inducer increases LacY production. This reinforcing cycle is positive feedback.

Uptake, production and loss

The two state variables are normalized internal TMG \(x\) and LacY expression \(y\), measured on a fluorescence scale. External TMG \(T\) remains fixed during each trajectory. The model balances uptake against internal loss and LacY production against protein loss:

\[ \frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{\beta y-x}{\tau_x} \] \[ \frac{dy}{dt}= \frac{\alpha(1+x^2)/(\rho+x^2)-y}{\tau_y}. \]

Here \(\beta=0.123T^{0.6}\) controls uptake, \(\alpha\) sets the production scale, and \(\rho\) sets repression strength. The parameters are \(\alpha=100.5\), \(\rho=167.1\) and \(\tau_x=\tau_y=1\); time is illustrative.

A stable state attracts nearby trajectories. The solid branches in the figure show stable expression levels; the dashed branch is unstable, so small deviations grow away from it. At the same external TMG level, the two starting states approach different stable expression levels.
